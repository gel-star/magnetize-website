The gap

Why does every channel look like it’s working?

Every channel marks its own homework but none know what a customer is worth to you.

  • Paid searchhits its cost-per-lead target, but the leads don’t become customers.
  • SEOgrows traffic from people researching, not buying.
  • Paid socialfinds the cheapest clicks, which are rarely your best customers.
  • AI searchgets judged on being seen, not on what being seen is worth.

Every channel looks good. Your P&L doesn’t move.

We’ve seen it in our own work

For one client, form fills rose throughout the year and every metric looked strong. But the business told us performance was down. They cared about contacts and bookings, and those were falling. The account data wasn’t wrong; it was measuring the wrong thing.

Why does nobody catch it?

Too much moves at once for one view to see it: your other channels, what happens on the phone after the click, competitor pricing, the economy, and whether your team can serve the demand you’ve just bought. Four agencies, four scoreboards, and none looking at what you are.

Meanwhile, you’re in a board meeting explaining why spending is up, but revenue isn’t. The reports say it’s working. The P&L says it isn’t. Both are true and hard to explain.

No amount of channel skill fixes a plan that was wrong before you spent a pound. That isn’t a channel problem. It’s a commercial one.

Rob EvansFounder & MD

Who we’re for

Why do regulated and considered markets need this most?

Because in these markets, the feedback loop everyone else relies on doesn’t exist.

A fashion brand sees the sale in the account minutes after the click. You don’t. Your enquiry becomes a consultation, then a conversation six weeks later over the phone. The channel never sees the outcome, so it optimises toward the nearest thing it can see and calls that success.

Then there’s the spread. A patient enquiry worth £80 and one worth £4,000 look identical in an ad account. Without the commercial layer, marketing spends the same to win both.

You can’t just outspend the problem. What you’re allowed to say, who you can target, and which data you can use are decided by someone else.

The two kinds of market we’re built for

Regulated

The rules decide what you can do before strategy gets a say. Certification requirements, restricted targeting, limited conversion data, and claims you can’t make even when they’re true.

Considered

Nobody buys on impulse. High value, long decision, several people involved, and the sale often finishes somewhere your ad account can’t see.

Plenty of our clients are both.

Questions

Questions people ask us

What is a commercial foundations review?

It’s a 30-minute free call where we determine what your marketing should produce before anyone touches an account. We assess what a customer is worth to you, what you can afford to pay to win one, whether your current targets match what you make, and what’s changed in the business that marketing hasn’t caught. You get our honest read on whether those targets hold up and what we’d fix first. There’s no obligation to continue, and if your money is better spent elsewhere for now, we’ll say so.

Why do you measure profit on ad spend rather than return on ad spend?

Because return on ad spend treats every pound of revenue as if it were worth the same, which it never is. A sale on a high-margin product and one on a low-margin product look identical in a ROAS figure, as do a lead that becomes a customer and one that wastes an hour of your sales team’s time. Profit on ad spend asks what the business kept. That’s the number your P&L moves on, so it’s the number we set targets against.

Do you work with ecommerce businesses, or only lead generation?

Both. Our three largest clients are ecommerce businesses, and about a third of our revenue comes from work outside lead generation. The method is the same, but it asks a different question: instead of what you can afford to pay for a lead, we determine which products are worth winning by accounting for margin, returns, and repeat buyers.

Can you guarantee we’ll appear in AI search answers?

No, and nobody can. Assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, and Google’s AI results decide what to name on their own. There is no place to buy and no setting to switch on. What we can do is build the evidence these systems look for: clear claims, specific numbers, content they can read, and measure whether you start appearing. We know the work matters because a conveyancing client found us this way: they asked Claude for an agency, and we were the answer.

How do you handle our data?

Your data stays yours and is not used to train any AI model. Before we connect anything, we show you exactly what Magnetize Pulse touches and what it does not, so you can take that to whoever needs to approve it. It’s the first question regulated businesses ask us, and it should be.

Which marketing channels do you run?

Paid search on Google and Microsoft, paid social on Meta and LinkedIn, SEO, AI search visibility, and ChatGPT Ads. We rarely recommend all of them and almost never all at once. The foundations review decides which ones your business needs and in what order.
